# Determine trademark and attribution requirements

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What trademark attribution, fan-product disclaimer, image-use, or metadata guidance from authoritative Pokémon sources should the public Pullpage catalog specification account for, without attempting to provide legal advice?

## Answer

Authoritative Pokémon sources provide no general fan-product or commercial reuse permission Pullpage can rely on. Pokémon Support asks projects not to use or associate Pokémon characters, names, and designs without permission, and Pokemon.com’s Terms do not grant commercial reuse rights in official site/card assets. An attribution line, trademark symbol, watermark, or “fan-made/unofficial” disclaimer therefore must not be treated as permission.

The specification must make a documented, territory-appropriate rights review a production launch gate for both the extended-art products and their Product Previews. Until qualified counsel approves the actual use in Indonesia and other served territories, implementation may use dummy or independently rights-cleared placeholders, but production content must not launch.

Required catalog controls are:

- make Pullpage the dominant source identity and prohibit unsupported “official,” “licensed,” “authorized,” partner, sponsorship, or endorsement claims;
- prohibit official logos, card backs, set symbols, character art, and similar assets as site chrome unless explicitly authorized;
- use Card Name, Set Name, Collector Number, and Rarity factually and only as needed to identify the depicted card, while keeping the Pullpage Catalog Product/Design Name distinct;
- do not scrape or copy Pokemon.com images or metadata merely because they are public;
- have counsel approve the exact global non-affiliation/trademark notice rather than copying Pokemon.com’s own footer as though it were a license;
- maintain rights provenance for each Product Preview (source, creator, owner/licensor, permission evidence, territory/channel, attribution, expiry, status, reviewer, and takedown note);
- allow publication only when that rights record is approved and current, with publisher validation failing closed;
- keep a prompt takedown/replacement path and avoid exposing source or print-ready artwork.

A candidate notice may be drafted for legal review, but the specification must label it unapproved until counsel verifies the entities, ownership wording, territory, and any copyright attribution. This research does not decide nominative use, fair use/fair dealing, first sale, derivative-work status, or Indonesian legal rights.
